## 24. Installation, activation, rollback, delete

## 25. Brain security

Package hashes; digital signatures verified against a key in the registry and
never against one that arrived with the package; zip-slip protection with
symlink members rejected outright; decompression-bomb protection decided from
declared sizes before any member is read; an **allowlist** of formats;
schema validation; no deserialization of arbitrary objects; a secret scanner
that never echoes the secret; a client-data scanner; tenant checks; audit;
quarantine.

A package from an untrusted signer may be inspected and evaluated and may not
be activated. Blocking at upload would stop a reviewer examining a package
they had every right to look at.

## 26. Regulatory Intelligence architecture

Analysis Studio owns the source library, the extracted requirements and
promotion into methods. AI Intelligence Studio owns what the bank learned
from them. §27 forbids conflating a source circular with a certified method,
and the failure that prevents is a bank telling its regulator that a rule is
implemented when what happened is that somebody uploaded a PDF.

## 27. Regulatory extraction and review

15 requirement types, 26 credit topics, a 16-stage pipeline that refuses a
skipped stage, and 7 review actions each needing a reason — approval
included. A clause matching no credit cue is **AMBIGUOUS**, never
NOT_CREDIT_RELATED: `classify()` has no code path to that value at all, and
only a named reviewer's REJECT — NOT RELEVANT sets it.

Interpretation confidence is computed from the evidence the extraction
actually had, with the sentences behind it, so a reviewer looking at 0.45
sees which four things were missing.

## 28. Regulatory conflicts

## 39. Raw feedback does not automatically alter production

**Confirmed, and enforced rather than promised.** A thumbs-down may change at
most two presentation preferences — the chart/table form and the answer
length — and `immediate()` has no argument that widens that. Every other
field becomes a Learning Ledger entry at CAPTURED and NON_PORTABLE, which is
where §42's ten-step governed path *starts*. Feedback cannot move a
validation score, an assurance status, a plan, a result, a release, a prompt,
a routing policy, a model, the ontology or a method.

## 40. The AI Brain is not Claude model weights

**Confirmed.** A Brain Pack contains no weights, no fine-tune, no gradient
and no checkpoint. It contains an ontology, teaching cases, blueprints,
policies, prompts, routing *roles* (never model identifiers), agent
contracts, approved regulatory requirements and method definitions — all as
JSON, JSONL, Markdown or CSV. Nothing in a package executes.

## 41. Measurable lift

**Unproven, and stated as unproven.** No Brain has been imported into this
installation, so the Lift Lab has nothing real to measure. What is
demonstrated is that the measurement is honest when it runs: §61's arithmetic
reproduces exactly, thin evidence returns INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE rather than a
number, a sender's holdout measures nothing, and one critical regression
overrides a positive average.

## 42. 99.99% accepted-answer precision

**Not demonstrated, and statistically unprovable at the sample sizes
available.** Establishing one failure in ten thousand needs tens of thousands
of adjudicated cases; this installation has 1,280 canonical cases and a
320-case sealed holdout. The product does not claim the figure anywhere, and
the Continuous Learning layer refuses to report any percentage below 30 cases
and refuses to show one at all below 12.

## 43. No live Anthropic calls, no credits consumed

**Confirmed.** No Anthropic API call was made at any point in this phase. No
API key was requested, read, printed or logged. Every test and every gate ran
against deterministic fixtures and fake providers. `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is
unset in this environment, which is why the live suites skip rather than
attempt a call with a placeholder.

# Extended additions (§93)

## 44. Continuous Learning architecture

§56's three things, kept apart: capture is continuous, activation is
governed, measurement is continuous and versioned. `backend/continuous/`
holds the third and has no path to the second — there is no function in it
that activates anything.

## 45. Baseline schema

§57's fields, in `learning_baselines`. Mostly versions, because "compared to
what?" is answered by an ontology version and a case-set version, not by a
date. `validate_baseline()` refuses one with no build (a comparison that
cannot separate "we improved" from "we deployed") or no development-set
version (comparing two different case sets and calling the difference
improvement).

## 46. Performance Snapshot schema

§59's fields, in `learning_snapshots`, append-only. Every development figure
has its validation twin in the next column: development is the set that was
tuned against and always looks better, so storing one of each pair would be
storing the flattering one.

## 47. Development vs validation methodology

Development is tuned against; validation is not, and `tuning_allowed()`
refuses it in as many words. Where the two disagree the dimension verdict is
MIXED and the sentence says the validation figure is the one to believe.

## 48. Sealed holdout separation

§58's six audiences are named and refused individually, and an *unrecognised*
audience is refused too — a typo in a caller should not open the holdout.
`aggregate()` filters to an allowlist rather than removing known-bad keys,
because a blocklist is a list of the field names somebody thought of.
Certification does not open it: certification establishes that a score is
meaningful, not that the exam may be circulated.

## 49. Cadence

11 triggers, split into those that follow a **change** (release, Brain
import, regulatory release, feedback batch, model change) and those that
follow the **clock**. A snapshot taken because something changed is worth
comparing against; a daily one taken while nothing happened is a data point
about noise, and the trigger column is how to tell them apart.

## 50 / 51. Learning quantity and quality

Two blocks, never one number. Where capture rose with no measured lift the
headline is §63's own sentence: **MORE KNOWLEDGE CAPTURED — NO MEASURED
PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT YET.**

## 52. Six-dimension improvement metrics

Both partitions per dimension, with case counts, coverage, critical
fixes/regressions, an evidence level and one of §62's six labels — including
STALE, which is a different statement from UNCHANGED.

## 53. Percentage-point / relative / error-reduction methodology

All three, always together. Points is what to quote, relative is what a
vendor quotes, error reduction is what an engineer cares about. Reporting
only the relative figure is how a 2 pp move on a small base becomes "a 40%
improvement" — which the test suite asserts explicitly.

## 54. Learning timeline

Every snapshot in order, marked by whether it followed a change or a
schedule.

## 55. Learning velocity

Captured, approved and activated as **separate rates**. Forty observations a
week with nothing activated is a backlog, and the capture rate alone reads as
a healthy learning system.

## 56. Change attribution

Named as belief rather than cause unless a change-isolation experiment
established it. A list of what happened in the same window is a list of
suspects.

## 57. Overfitting detection

§76's worked example — development +8 pp, validation +0.5 pp — returns
POSSIBLE OVERFITTING. Validation regressing while development improves is
flagged as the clearest form. A critical validation regression blocks
activation outright: it is a wrong answer the bank would have shown a client,
and an average does not make it not one.

## 58–61. Attribution by source

§78's seven sources, and only those measured in isolation are attributed. The
rest go into **UNATTRIBUTED / INTERACTION**, which is why the waterfall does
not always balance — a waterfall that always balances is one somebody made
balance, and the made-up bar is the one a reader trusts most because it makes
the picture work.

## 62. Learning Report

§83's twenty sheets, all present even when six are empty: a report whose shape
changes with its content cannot be compared to last quarter's. The empty ones
say what would have been there. The workbook refuses to save if it carries a
secret or a client row, scanning the assembled cells rather than the inputs.

## 63. Continuous Learning tests

signal, the waterfall, the windows, staleness and the report.

## 67. Statistical / evidence strength

## 68. Increased case count is not performance improvement

**Confirmed, and enforced.** `quality_verdict()` reports quantity and quality
as separate blocks; the cockpit renders them as two cards with a sentence

anywhere that produces a single number from both.

## 69. Sealed-holdout isolation

**Confirmed.** No sealed-holdout question, gold answer or case identifier
appears in any package, any screen, any report or any API response. The
holdout's *version* is recorded, which says which exam was sat without
circulating it. Isolation is asserted by `holdout.assert_isolated()` —
fingerprint, cluster and question disjointness — and by the partition tests.
