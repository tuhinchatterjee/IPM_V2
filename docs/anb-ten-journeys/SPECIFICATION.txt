2. Keep the financial reasoning defensible
“Make the problem grave” means construct material adverse synthetic episodes with honest controls, amounts, persistence and severity arithmetic. It does not mean exaggerate axes, invent causality, deteriorate every model variable or claim that High EWS customers will certainly default.
Affected population
Evaluate the case predicate at the declared date, count facilities or distinct customers according to case grain. Show eligible count as denominator.
Do not: Stock is not migration; a stock rise alone cannot identify current→arrears flows.
Roll rate
Same-facility transition from beginning bucket to ending bucket divided by eligible starting-bucket facilities, excluding/labeling closures and new entrants.
Do not: Do not infer from the difference between two stock percentages.
ODR
Observed first default events over a named matured window / original at-risk population. C02 uses MOB6 cumulative first default; C10 uses six-month redefault.
Do not: This is not PD, EWS or a 1–29 stock rate; windows are not interchangeable.
PD12 / lifetime PD
Approved model, horizon, point-in-time/through-cycle and scenario basis explicit. Compare the same eligible non-impaired cohort, preferably EAD-weighted.
Do not: Already-impaired accounts are not new forward-risk predictions. Show their stage separately and N/A where a comparable forward PD does not apply.
Application score
Immutable original score, input features, decision policy/model and original date.
Do not: A change in portfolio average can be mix change; do not call it the same customer’s original score deterioration.
Behavioral score
Longitudinal same-customer observations under comparable model versions; lower/worse convention stated.
Do not: Separate the DPD input from other drivers and verify temporal ordering.
EWS
Governed score/band/reasons at customer-product grain and declared date. Expose model coverage and risk horizon if validated.
Do not: High EWS is elevated risk, not certainty of default or automatically DPD 0.
Current bad / forward risk
Use the approved rule definitions. This fixture uses 30+ DPD for current bad and High/Critical outside that state for forward risk.
Do not: Do not subtract inconsistent card counts and call the remainder all DPD-0 customers.
ECL
Approved stage-aware, probability-weighted discounted loss with appropriate term/cash-flow data.
Do not: A three-factor one-period proxy is not a universal IFRS9 calculation.
EAD
Drawn exposure plus eligible expected future drawings under CCF/behavioral exposure assumptions.
Do not: A limit cut does not repay existing drawn principal.
Concentration
Show share of eligible book, share of cases, within-pocket incidence and outside-pocket incidence; include sample size.
Do not: Large population count alone does not imply high risk; control for age/vintage and confounding.
Severity
Existing governed materiality plus persistence/excess-risk and confidence rules; retain the arithmetic in Trace.
Do not: No threshold change merely to turn a demo card red; no repeated duplicate cases to reach ten.
Recent-card ODR
Store value, numerator, denominator, horizon and source snapshot in the saved investigation.
Do not: For an early case with no completed outcome window, show Not yet observed rather than fabricate a default rate.
Missingness
Coverage count and explicit reason for unavailable, stale, unverified or inapplicable data.
Do not: Do not fill missing income, EWS or PD with zero or reuse an unrelated cohort.
2.1 Specific modeling requirements
Use original application scorecards for underwriting-quality and vintage cases; use behavioral models for post-origination changes. For each, expose every available model input and its contribution, not only the two strongest variables. Collapse minor variables visually only with an expandable full table. Distinguish raw-variable change from score points or SHAP units. Contribution totals must reconcile to the reference/ending score or display the unexplained residual. Never manufacture points for a feature that the actual model does not use.
Compare the same IDs through time. Show eligibility, missingness and model-version changes. Application scores are immutable at decision time: an average can change because the population mix changes, not because the original score was retroactively rescored. If behavioral model versions changed, either use a valid bridge or display non-comparability rather than subtracting incompatible scores.
Separate statement-cycle mechanics from credit stress. Historical minimum or partial payments are not themselves an overdue-payment event if the required minimum was met. In C01 the repayment feature is a trailing-cycle behavior measure; the later missed required payment establishes DPD. Require dated source evidence to support the sequence. Low cash buffers or absent salary credits must be verified against authorized alternative explanations.
For vintage ODR, match months-on-book and retain the original at-risk denominator, including paid-off or closed borrowers when appropriate. For a roll-rate calculation, track the same starting facilities and label new business, closures, write-offs, cures and exits. Never infer current→arrears transitions from the difference between two stock percentages alone.
2.2 ECL and simulation integrity
Production ECL must use the installed governed methodology: stage/SICR/default definitions, scenario probabilities, conditional and marginal PD term structure, survival, EAD/CCF path, conditional LGD and recoveries, effective-interest/profit-rate discounting, remaining contractual/behavioral horizon and any governed overlays. Preserve approved modifications and staging policy. A simple PD × LGD × EAD display can explain a component, but cannot substitute for the full IFRS9 calculation. [R07]
The workbook includes thirty formula proofs with compact annual buckets and 1,000 published synthetic model outputs. These prove the arithmetic of the fixture, not regulatory compliance or calibration quality. Do not truncate a real mortgage’s horizon to the teaching example’s three annual buckets. Stage 1 uses default events in the next twelve months and their lifetime loss, not only cash losses occurring in those twelve months. Credit-impaired records use recovery/cash-shortfall logic and must not be presented as fresh forward-risk predictions.
For existing revolving exposure, reducing a credit limit does not repay drawn principal. Model the lawful remaining undrawn headroom and CCF, and do not push a proposed limit below existing drawings without an explicit contractual/treatment analysis. Separate future-origination limits from existing accounts. A reschedule may increase duration or ECL; a validated LGD or stage correction may increase the allowance. Show the adverse tradeoff rather than forcing every action to improve the headline loss.
2.3 Policy hierarchy and approval
Apply the hierarchy: applicable law/regulation → bank-approved effective policy → delegated authority and customer contract → proposed action. Public SAMA guidance is not a substitute for the bank’s proprietary thresholds. Rules depend on income band, salary/pension status, obligation category and housing-support eligibility, so no blanket 45%, 55% or 65% limit may be applied indiscriminately. Collection-rule default and finance-model default must remain distinct definitions where applicable. [R01–R03]
No approved bank policy was supplied with this request. Search authorized repository/document sources for it. If unavailable, implement the DEMO_DRAFT templates in Policy_Actions, prominently labeled NOT BANK APPROVED. Every generated recommendation must carry policy ID, version, clause, effective date, applicability, evidence, owner, sequence, deadline, approval requirement and safeguard. Do not claim compliance or execute actions based on a draft template.
For public-rule checks, revalidate the current source at implementation and record effective dates. Do not embed commercial product terms or superseded BNPL caps as immutable law. The December 2025 onward regulatory landscape is one reason to keep source/version metadata rather than a single hardcoded debt-burden threshold. [R06]
If an approved clause defines an exposure or ECL budget, calculate a candidate action by solving the governed scenario engine within contractual, affordability and policy bounds, and disclose the objective, constraints and feasibility. For an existing card, the unchanged drawn balance can make the requested loss target infeasible. Without an approved budget, the 15%, 20% and 25% new-limit reductions are explicit scenario trials, not mathematically optimal recommendations. Do not reverse-engineer an arbitrary percentage and present it as a bank rule.

Product pages are context, not authority to recommend a specific customer action. At implementation, revalidate commercial terms, consolidated rules and bank-approved internal clauses. URLs are included in the workbook Sources sheet for review; no customer information is derived from public pages.
R01 — SAMA | Responsible Lending Principles for Individual Customers
2018 consolidated rules; accessed 17 Sep 2026. Documented underwriting, credit-history verification, disposable-income assessment and treatment of uneven installments. Public rules do not establish actual bank portfolio deterioration.
https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/responsible-lending-principles-individual-customers-0
R02 — SAMA | Chapter IV: Quantitative Principles of Responsible Lending
Accessed 17 Sep 2026. Affordability limits depend on income, salary/pension, obligation type and supported-housing status. Never apply one universal debt-burden threshold to every customer.
https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/chapter-iv-quantitative-principles-responsible-lending
R03 — SAMA | Debt Collection Regulations and Procedures
In-force March 2025 rules; accessed 17 Sep 2026. Articles 4, 7–10 cover confidential contact, payment-date alignment, deductions and responses to changed circumstances. The operational definition of default here is not automatically the IFRS9/model definition.
https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/debt-collection-regulations-and-procedures
R04 — Arab National Bank | Auto Lease product information
Public product context; terms must be revalidated at implementation. Final-payment and used-vehicle structures provide context for maturity and recovery cases. Refinancing availability is not a promise of approval. No ANB loss or default figures are asserted.
https://anb.com.sa/web/anb/auto-lease
R05 — Arab National Bank | Real Estate Finance product information
Public product context; page availability/terms must be revalidated. Self-construction, housing-support and repayment-structure context. Synthetic delays and support mismatches below are invented; do not infer a real developer or support programme failure.
https://anb.com.sa/web/anb/real-estate
R06 — SAMA | Rules for Regulating Buy-Now-Pay-Later Companies
Consolidated rules; accessed 17 Sep 2026. Credit-record and repayment-capacity requirements inform external-obligation verification. Actual bureau coverage, consent and reporting lag must be checked. Do not hard-code an obsolete BNPL cap.
https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/rules-regulating-buy-now-pay-later-bnpl-companies-0
R07 — IFRS Foundation | IFRS 9 Financial Instruments / impairment background
Accessed 17 Sep 2026. Probability-weighted, time-sensitive impairment measurement and distinction between 12-month and lifetime losses. The teaching calculation in this workbook is not an approved bank methodology.
https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/
R08 — SDAIA | Personal Data Protection knowledge centre
Accessed 17 Sep 2026. Privacy context for purpose-limited customer investigations, controlled exports and retention. Bank legal/compliance must specify the lawful basis and concrete retention/transfer rules.
https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter
